Microsoft — Phi family

Microsoft Research small-model line built on the "Textbooks Are All You Need" thesis (synthetic + filtered web data, <15B params). Phi-1 (Jun 2023) → Phi-4 (Dec 2024, 14B dense) → Phi-4-reasoning-plus (Apr 2025), the cited reasoning flagship; Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B (Mar 2026) is the newest with hybrid <think>/<nothink> self-selection. Allen Institute (AI2) — OLMo family
